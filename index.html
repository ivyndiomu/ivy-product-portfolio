How I work

Product thinking shaped by physical delivery

My engineering and construction background pushes me to treat constraints, sequencing, dependencies and failure states as part of the product, not implementation details.

Find the real job

Move past the visible request and map the complete workflow, including what happens before and after the screen.

Make trade-offs explicit

Separate must-work production logic from impressive but lower-value features. A useful flat preview can beat a weak 3D simulation.

Protect correctness

When software drives physical work, data models, migration and regression tests become product-quality decisions.

Current direction

Construction management × product management

I am building toward product roles where digital systems coordinate real-world work, especially construction technology, technical products and AI-enabled workflows.
