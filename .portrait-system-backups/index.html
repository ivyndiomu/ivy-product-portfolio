03 / Working method

Editorial clarity. Engineered structure.

My approach comes from work where software decisions have to survive real operations, constraints and handoffs.

01 / Frame

Find the real job

Map what happens before, during and after the visible request.

02 / Define

Make the system explicit

Turn ambiguity into requirements, decision rules, priorities and acceptance criteria.

03 / Protect

Design for correctness

When software drives physical work, failure states and data integrity are product decisions.

04 / Learn

Use delivery as evidence

Observe what survives contact with real users, real workflows and real constraints.

Software is most interesting to me when it has to respect the physical world.
