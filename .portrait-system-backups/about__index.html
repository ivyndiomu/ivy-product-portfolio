01 / About

A career shaped by systems that have to survive execution.

Through-line

Civil engineering → construction operations → product management → independent product building.

The through-line

My work has changed industries and interfaces, but the underlying question has stayed remarkably consistent: how do you turn a complex operating reality into a system that people can execute reliably?

Engineering taught me to respect constraints. Construction taught me that sequencing, dependencies and field conditions can invalidate a beautiful plan. Product management gave me a framework for turning those lessons into software: research, requirements, prioritisation, validation, delivery and measurement.

That is why I am drawn to technical products, construction technology, B2B SaaS and AI-enabled workflows. I like products where the work is complicated before the interface ever appears.
