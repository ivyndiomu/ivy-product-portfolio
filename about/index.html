About

I learned product through systems that have to work in the real world.

My path runs from civil engineering to construction management to product management, with crochet and independent product building as another place to test how digital decisions become physical outcomes.

One through-line

Construction taught me that a plan is only valuable if it survives execution. Product management gave me a structured way to turn messy workflows, constraints and user needs into systems that can scale beyond one person's memory.

Crochet looks different from construction, but the product questions are familiar: dimensions, sequencing, material choices, tolerances, handoffs, quality checks and customer expectations.

That is why I am particularly interested in construction technology, technical product management and AI-enabled products that coordinate real work rather than existing only inside a screen.
